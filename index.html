About Me

Where Traditional Art
Meets Digital Craft.

I'm Prajwal Sharma — a visual storyteller operating at the precise intersection of traditional artistry and cutting-edge digital design. My work is deeply rooted in the discipline of pencil portraiture, where years of mastering value, form, and composition laid the foundation for a highly analytical design sensibility.

Today, I translate that same precision into brand identities that command attention and UI/UX experiences that feel inevitable. Every pixel, every typeface pairing, every spatial decision is intentional — because great design isn't decoration. It's strategy.
